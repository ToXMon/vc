    How to Play

    1. Objective

    Be the first player to collect three green chips in a row, or collect the most chips when time runs out.

    2. Dice Rolling

    On your turn, roll three dice: green, red, and yellow. The combination determines your action.

    3. Chip Collection

    Green chips: Advance toward victory
    Red chips: Block opponents
    Heart chips: Protection and bonuses

    4. Winning

    Get three green chips in a row or have the most chips at the end!
